# 🍀 Fortuna · Lucky Number Studio

Lucky numbers from numerology and real lottery history. Open `index.html` in any browser. No sign-up, works offline.

## 📖 How to use

1. Stay on the **🎲 Pick** tab.
2. Set **Numbers** (how many you want, default 4), **From** and **To**, or tap a quick range such as 1–49.
3. Tap **Reveal my numbers**. Each number comes with a short numerology reading.
4. Tap **Copy** to copy your numbers, or **📤 Share as image** to save or send them.
5. For a more personal draw, open **⚙️ More options** and add your birth date, full name or an intention (Money, Love, Health, Career, Luck).
6. To always play certain numbers, type them in **Lock numbers**. To avoid some, type them in **Exclude numbers**.
7. Tap **⭐ Apply best-value pattern** to switch on the smart options in one tap.
8. After a draw, open **✅ Check**, enter the winning numbers and tap **Check my sets & save draw** to see how many numbers matched.
9. Open **📊 Patterns** to see the lottery data behind the numbers. Tap a card title to open it. Tap a TOTO pair or triplet to lock it into your next draw.
10. **Install it on your phone** (needs the https link from GitHub Pages, not the file opened from your phone's storage):
    - Tap **🤖 Install on Android** or **🍎 Install on iPhone** at the top of the app.
    - **Android (Chrome):** the button opens the install box right away. If it doesn't, open the ⋮ menu and choose **Install app**.
    - **iPhone (Safari):** Apple doesn't allow one-tap installs, so the button shows the steps: **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
    - After the first visit it opens full screen from your home screen and works offline.
11. When every number in a range has been used, Fortuna starts a fresh round automatically.

## ⚠️ Warnings

- **No prediction.** Lottery draws are random and independent. No number, pattern, streak or algorithm can predict a draw or improve your chance of winning, and adding past results does not change that.
- **Patterns only change how your numbers look.** Hot numbers, winning series and real-draw patterns are history. "Beat the crowd" only makes sharing a prize less likely, not winning more likely.
- **Check your real odds.** Open **Patterns → Your real odds** to see the exact chance for your setup. Every combination has the same chance.
- **Data is approximate.** Lists were compiled in November 2026 from public websites and could not be verified at the source. Sources use different periods and formats, so counts cannot be compared directly. Always check official results with your lottery operator.
- **Numerology is for fun.** The meanings are entertainment, not advice.
- **Not affiliated.** Fortuna is not connected to any lottery operator and does not sell or buy tickets.
- **Play responsibly.** Only spend what you can afford to lose, and only if you are of legal age where you live. If gambling stops being fun, contact a local support service.
- **Updating the installed app.** If you change any file, raise the version number in `sw.js` (`fortuna-v2` to `fortuna-v3`) so phones pick up the update.
- **Your data stays in your browser.** Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app erases your history and saved draws. Use **Export** on the Check tab to back them up.
