🌍 World lottery patterns

World hot numbers (appear near the top of the most lists)

What real draws look like (exact maths for a 6-from-49 game)

  • The average sum of the six numbers is 150, and 87% of all combinations add up to between 100 and 200.
  • Mixed odd and even is normal: 3 odd + 3 even is the most common split (33%), then 4/2 (25%) and 2/4 (23%). All odd or all even is only about 1% each.
  • About half of all draws (49.5%) contain at least one consecutive pair, like 23 and 24.
  • Only 5% of combinations are entirely 31 or below, yet many players pick birthdays. Higher numbers make a shared jackpot less likely.

Honest note: hot numbers are history, not prediction. Every draw is independent and every number has the same chance. Counts come from different sources, periods and formats, so they can’t be compared directly. The same six numbers came up in two draws in a row. 18 people matched all six in the second draw. Officials investigated and called it a coincidence.
Korea 6/45: an “all 20s” draw
Bonus ball 6. 36 people won first prize. Only 210 of 8,145,060 possible sets are all in the 20s, about 1 draw in 38,786.

Fun link: 4 and 23 from Bulgaria’s repeated set are also TOTO’s most common pair (5 times). Any single set repeating is rare, but with so many lotteries drawing so often, some coincidence is expected somewhere.
